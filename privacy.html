Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to Oops! ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information regarding players and visitors who access our online HTML5 web game hosted at our website.

1. Information We Collect

Oops! is designed as a client-side instant web game. We do not require account registration or collect personal identifying information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) to play the game.

  • Local Gameplay Data: We store level progression, completed worlds, death counts, and audio preferences locally in your browser using standard Web Storage (localStorage). This data stays strictly on your device.
  • Optional Player Feedback: If you voluntarily submit feedback or bug reports through our in-game feedback form, we collect the feedback message, category, and optional nickname you provide to improve gameplay.
  • Device & Browser Information: When you access our website, standard web server logs may record basic non-personal technical data such as browser type, operating system, language preference, referring site, and timestamp.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Partners

We may partner with Google AdSense (including Google H5 Game Ads and Ad Placement API) and third-party advertising networks to display relevant advertisements (such as optional rewarded video ads to skip difficult levels).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting AboutAds.info.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use essential local storage for saving game progress and performance preferences. Third-party advertising partners may place cookies on your browser for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, and fraud prevention.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Our game is a family-friendly puzzle platformer. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

5. GDPR and CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area or California), you have rights regarding your data, including the right to access, rectify, or delete any data we maintain, as well as the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage players to review this page occasionally.

7. Contact Us
