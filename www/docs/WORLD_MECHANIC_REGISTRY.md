# 📜 OOPS! — World Mechanic Registry & Future Design Space

This registry serves as the definitive design contract across all chapters of **OOPS!**.
To uphold the **No Repetition Policy**, every world must have a distinct mechanical identity. No world may duplicate or merely reskin the primary mechanics of an earlier world.

---

## 🏜️ WORLD 1: DESERT RUINS
* **Chapter Identity**: Precision platforming and classic deceptive trap design.
* **Core Philosophy**: *"Trust nothing you see on the surface."*
* **Exclusive Mechanics**:
  1. **Sinking Sandstone**: Platforms that shake and fall upon weight contact with progressive delay.
  2. **Hidden Pop-Up Spikes**: Spikes flush with ground tiles that trigger on player proximity or landing.
  3. **Falling Steel Crushers**: Heavy weighted blocks that slam down when the player enters their drop column, requiring bait-and-dash maneuvers.
  4. **Spring Trampolines**: Fixed springboards providing high vertical launch trajectories.
  5. **Fleeing Exit Portals**: Exit doors that hop or teleport away when approached blindly.
  6. **Ceiling Spikes & Pit Traps**: Classic retro platformer hazards.

---

## 🌌 WORLD 2: THE SHIFT
* **Chapter Identity**: 2.5D reality distortion, temporal manipulation, and self-cooperation.
* **Core Philosophy**: *"A beautiful world that slowly becomes wrong."*
* **Exclusive Mechanics**:
  1. **Mirror World (Levels 1–10)**:
     - Mirrored platform planes and reverse reflection paths.
     - Phantom platforms (structures that only exist in reflection, or look solid in reflection but are ethereal in real space).
     - Inverted input crystals and mirror-flipped traversal.
  2. **Time Zones (Levels 11–20)**:
     - Localized **Slow Zones** (0.35× physics timescale for moving obstacles, falling hazards, and platforms).
     - Localized **Fast Zones** (1.8× physics timescale requiring rapid rhythm navigation).
     - Moving world architecture and rotating platforms.
  3. **Echo / Shadow Player (Levels 21–30)**:
     - Automated time-delayed ghost recorder (replays player's movement trajectory 2.0s later).
     - Echo activates pressure plates, remote switches, and opens locked doors for the live player.
     - Collaborative single-player time-loop puzzles.
  4. **Magnetic Mechanics (Levels 31–40)**:
     - North/South magnetic attraction and repulsion fields.
     - Polarized metallic blocks that can be repelled across gaps or drawn toward nodes.
     - Magnetic surface orientation locks.
  5. **Multi-Switch Logic Circuits (Levels 31–40)**:
     - Interlinked circuits (Switch A unlocks Barrier B, which powers Platform C).
  6. **Reality Glitches & Atmospheric Anomalies (Levels 41–44)**:
     - Environmental storytelling without text: vanishing background monuments, eerie audio distortion, chromatic aberration, flickering shadows.
  7. **The Reality Shatter & Reveal (Level 45)**:
     - Freeze, silence, geometric collapse, alien portal appearance, and a **15–20 second cinematic teaser for World 3**.
  8. **The Descent & Climax (Levels 46–50)**:
     - Unstable composite levels combining all four World 2 mechanics into a grand finale.

## 🔒 WORLD 3: (RESERVED — THE QUANTUM VOID / MONOLITH)
* **Status**: In Pre-Production
* **Tentative Theme**: Cosmic monoliths, dimensional tearing, non-Euclidean space.
* **Reserved Mechanics (DO NOT USE IN WORLD 2)**:
  - Portal-to-portal continuous wormholes.
  - Direct gravity manipulation / 4-way arbitrary gravity inversion.
  - Scale shifting (player grows/shrinks).
  - Light vs Dark stealth and shadow projection mechanics.
  - Rewind / Time-scrubbing.

## 🔒 WORLD 4: (RESERVED — THE CYBER NEXUS)
* **Status**: Concept Phase
* **Tentative Theme**: Glitched cyberspace, data streams, digital decay.
* **Reserved Mechanics (DO NOT USE IN WORLD 2 OR 3)**:
  - Binary hacking terminals.
  - Phase-shifting between red/blue data layers.
  - Laser deflection prisms and optical mirrors.
  - Rhythm-synchronized beat hazards.
